# Mapdata Tab
The Mapdata tab of [PyDAT](/Help/Programs/PyDAT.md) edits [mapdata.dat](/Help/Files/DAT/mapdata.dat.md), which lists the games campaign missions.
Each entry has a single **Mission Dir** setting: the folder containing the missions [map](/Help/Files/Maps.md), referenced by its index in `mapdata.tbl` (the dropdown shows the paths).
