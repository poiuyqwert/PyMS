[Units editor]
#####BASIC TAB#####
UnitHP=Unit Hit Points (HP) or "life" amount. Stored in 1/256 HP (this field is the whole part, Fraction the rest). Values over "9999" will be accepted in-game, but they will not be displayed. A unit being built starts at a tenth of it. Map unit settings can replace it.

UnitHPFraction=Fractional portion of HP (0-255), in 1/256 HP. Instead of using decimals, SC uses a whole number and a fractional number. To represent "1.5" HP, you would use 1 for the HP, and 128 for the fraction.

UnitShieldEnable=Enables Shields for the unit. Works for any unit, not only Protoss. Without it the Shields amount is ignored: shields do not absorb damage, regenerate or show. Terran and zerg buildings with shields do NOT acquire full shield capacity during construction.

UnitSP=Maximum Shield Points, in whole points. Only used if shields are Enabled. Shields absorb damage before the unit's Hit Points and regenerate slowly. Values over "9999" hide the numbers in the status panel. Map unit settings can replace it.

UnitArmor=Unit's basic Armor, in whole points. Armor plus the Armor Upgrade level is subtracted from every hit that gets past the shields; weapons with the Ignore Armor damage type skip it. A hit that shields did not touch still does at least 0.5 HP. Map unit settings can replace it.

UnitArmorUpgrade=The upgrade whose level is added to the unit's Armor (any upgrade works). Its icon and name are shown for the armor in the status panel; upgrade 60 (None) hides the armor icon. [pointer to upgrades.dat]

UnitMinerals=Amount of minerals needed to train/build/morph into the unit. Zerglings and Scourge pay once per pair. Cancelling a unit in training or an egg refunds it all; cancelling a building under construction or a Zerg building morph refunds 75%. Map unit settings can replace it.

UnitVespene=Amount of Vespene Gas needed to train/build/morph into the unit, paid and refunded like minerals. Map unit settings can replace it.

UnitTime=Amount of time it takes to train/build the unit, in game frames (1/24ths of a second at Fastest speed). A building gains its HP and shields over this time. A value of 0 is treated as 1, but a progress bar for the unit would divide by zero. Map unit settings can replace it.

UnitIsBW=Marks the unit as BroodWar-only for StarEdit, which only offers it on BroodWar maps and warns about it otherwise. The game itself ignores this; it uses the "BroodWar Only" StarEdit availability flag instead.

UnitWeaponGround=Weapon used against units on the ground (not flying). 130 = None; the unit then uses its Subunit 1's ground weapon, if it has one. The Lurker only has its ground weapon while burrowed. [pointer to weapons.dat]
UnitWeaponGroundList=Weapon used against units on the ground (not flying). 130 = None; the unit then uses its Subunit 1's ground weapon, if it has one. The Lurker only has its ground weapon while burrowed. [pointer to weapons.dat]

UnitGroundMax=Max number of times unit hits its target per Ground attack. This value is for statistics purposes only: the game never reads it. StarEdit shows the weapon's damage times this value times the weapon's damage factor (using the subunit's values if the unit has a subunit).

UnitWeaponAir=Weapon used against flying units. 130 = None; the unit then uses its Subunit 1's air weapon, if it has one. [pointer to weapons.dat]
UnitWeaponAirList=Weapon used against flying units. 130 = None; the unit then uses its Subunit 1's air weapon, if it has one. [pointer to weapons.dat]

UnitAirMax=Max number of times unit hits its target per Air attack. This value is for statistics purposes only: the game never reads it. StarEdit shows the weapon's damage times this value times the weapon's damage factor (using the subunit's values if the unit has a subunit).

UnitScoreBuild=Amount of points given to the owner when the unit is completed, counted as unit score (Men group) or structure score (Building group).

UnitScoreDestroy=Amount of points given for destroying the unit, counted to the total score after the end of a game. It is also used by the AI for targeting purposes. Units with a higher destroy score will be targeted first.

UnitSpaceProv=Amount of loading space the unit has; any value above 0 makes it a transport (Overlords also need the transport upgrade). This IS NOT the number of units it may carry, as different units take up different amounts of space. Values above 8 are unsafe: a transport has only 8 passenger slots.

UnitSpaceReq=Amount of loading space the unit takes up in a transport. If it is higher than the transport's free space, the unit cannot be loaded (255 = never). Bunkers only take Terran units with 1. The transport's status panel only shows passengers with 1, 2 or 4.

UnitSupProv=Amount of Supply/Psi/Control the unit adds to its race's pool (from its Zerg/Terran/Protoss flag) when completed, taken away when it is lost. The game counts in halves (the +0.5 box); displays round halves up. A race can have at most 200.
UnitSupProv05=Amount of Supply/Psi/Control the unit adds to its race's pool (from its Zerg/Terran/Protoss flag) when completed, taken away when it is lost. The game counts in halves (the +0.5 box); displays round halves up. A race can have at most 200.

UnitSupReq=Amount of Supply/Psi/Control required to train/build the unit, from its race's pool (Zerg/Terran/Protoss flag). The game counts in halves (the +0.5 box); displays round halves up. Doubled for "Two Units in One Egg". Not checked with "Ignore Supply Check".
UnitSupReq05=Amount of Supply/Psi/Control required to train/build the unit, from its race's pool (Zerg/Terran/Protoss flag). The game counts in halves (the +0.5 box); displays round halves up. Doubled for "Two Units in One Egg". Not checked with "Ignore Supply Check".

UnitSize=Sets how much damage the unit takes, by the weapon's damage type: Explosive (50% to Small, 75% to Medium, 100% to Large), Concussive (100% to Small, 50% to Medium, 25% to Large), Normal 100%. "Independent" (0) takes 0% from every weapon, so each hit only does the 0.5 HP minimum (nothing if shields took part of it). Values above 3 are not checked.

UnitSight=Sight range, in tiles (32 pixels), indicating how much Fog-Of-War the unit clears. Valid values are 0-11: the game looks the value up in a table of 12 sight shapes without checking it, so larger values read past the table and can CRASH the game. Buildings under construction see 4, blinded units 2.

UnitSEGroupProtoss=Unit is treated as a Protoss unit: race for its supply pool (Psi), race-specific sounds and messages, and status panel graphics. In StarEdit, the race decides which players may own it (unless "All Races" is set).

UnitSEGroupTerran=Unit is treated as a Terran unit: race for its supply pool, race-specific sounds and messages, and status panel graphics. Terran units show their Rank/Sublabel under their name. A damaged Terran building burns. In StarEdit, the race decides which players may own it.

UnitSEGroupZerg=Unit is treated as a Zerg unit: race for its supply pool (Control), race-specific sounds and messages, and status panel graphics. Used by Zerg creep placement rules. In StarEdit, the race decides which players may own it.

UnitTAR=How far, in tiles (32 pixels), the unit looks for targets to attack by itself. Used by every unit, not only turrets and Carriers/Reavers. When the game loads, it raises this to at least the unit's weapon ranges (weapons.dat range / 32), and a unit with a subunit uses the subunit's value. Range upgrades add to it.

#####ADVANCED TAB#####
UnitAdvAddon=Unit is a Terran add-on: placed and attached next to its parent building, at its Addon Position.

UnitAdvAnimIdle=The unit can be clicked on any clickable image of its sprite (overlays such as the Science Vessel's turret or the Archon's inner image), not only its main image. Nothing to do with idle animations.

UnitAdvAutoAttack="Can Turn": the unit can turn. It gets its starting Direction when completed and skips the facing check when it looks for targets. Turrets and static defences have it without "Can Move".

UnitAdvBattleReactions="Can Move": the unit can move. Without it the unit is a stationary object for pathing (buildings, turrets, sieged tanks, eggs).

UnitAdvBuilding=Unit is considered as a building for targeting purposes, and affects Addons-related properties. Also allows for other units to be built/trained 'inside' the unit and makes the unit placement dependent on the "Placement Box" property instead of the "Unit Dimensions".

UnitAdvBurrow=Allows the unit to use the Burrow button (the button's requirement checks this flag). It does NOT give/remove the "Burrow" button.

UnitAdvCloak=Does NOT let the unit cloak (that is decided by its buttons and orders). Only gives the "Group (Cloakers)" button set when every selected unit has it, lets the computer AI decloak the unit, and allows the "Cloaked" property in StarEdit.

UnitAdvReqCreep=Building MUST be built on creep, and creep is filled in under it when it is created.

UnitAdvResDepot=Makes a building (and ONLY a building) a place workers can return resources to. Also makes it impossible to place/land the building next to a unit with the "Resource Container" property.

UnitAdvDetector=Unit can detect cloaked enemy units (while completed and not disabled, locked down, in stasis, maelstromed or blinded) and receives the "Detector" title under its name.

UnitAdvFlyBuilding=Allows the building to lift off (to be in the "In Transit" or "Flying" state both in the game and in StarEdit), but it will crash if the unit does not have a Lift-off and Landing animations (in Iscript.bin). A damaged Terran unit with this flag burns. Does not give the unit its buttons.

UnitAdvFlyer=Unit is created flying: it is hit by Air weapons instead of Ground weapons. Only read when the unit is created. Whether it moves over terrain and other units like an air unit is set by its Elevation level (12 or more).

UnitAdvResContainer=Unit holds resources, minerals or gas (mineral fields, geysers and refineries). Gives the unit a resource amount (settable in StarEdit), and is used for harvesting and the status panel. Prevents "Resource Depots" from being located next to it.

UnitAdvHero=Unit is a hero: 250 maximum energy, full Carrier/Reaver hangar without the capacity upgrade, Ultralisk +2 armor without the armor upgrade, and hero wireframe colours. The computer does not target heroes with some spells.

UnitAdvIgnoreSupply=The unit is made without checking supply (used for morphed units such as the Guardian, and Zerg buildings); its supply is still added to the used count.

UnitAdvInvincible=Unit cannot be a target of any sort of weapons or abilities, and triggers cannot change its invincibility. It also hides the unit's Hit Points value.

UnitAdvMechanical=Unit is treated as a mechanical-type target for weapons and abilities (weapons.dat target flags Mechanical and Organic or Mechanical, e.g. Lockdown). It can also be repaired by SCVs.

UnitAdvSingleEntity=Unit is a powerup (Flag, Data Disc, Khaydarin Crystal, mineral and gas pickups) that workers carry. It cannot be selected with other units and is left out of collision, attacks, cloaking and giving to another player.

UnitAdvOrganic=Unit is treated as an organic-type target for weapons and abilities (weapons.dat target flags Organic and Organic or Mechanical, Maelstrom, Irradiate) and can be healed by Medics.

UnitAdvOverlayLarge=Unit will use large spell overlay graphics (Lockdown, Ensnare, Plague, Defensive Matrix etc.). Has nothing to do with the damage Unit Size. If both overlay flags are set, Medium wins.

UnitAdvOverlayMed=Unit will use medium spell overlay graphics (Lockdown, Ensnare, Plague, Defensive Matrix etc.). Has nothing to do with the damage Unit Size. With neither overlay flag the overlays are small.

UnitAdvPermaCloak=Unit cloaks when it is completed or warped in. If the unit is also a Spellcaster, giving this property will make it lose energy until 0.

UnitAdvPickup=Unused: nothing in the game or StarEdit tests this flag (it is set on the pickups 216-227).

UnitAdvProducesUnits=Larger creep: a Zerg town hall (Hatchery, Lair, Hive) with this flag gets a larger first creep patch when it is placed. Nothing to do with producing units.

UnitAdvReqPsi=Unit must be built within a PSI field, like that produced by pylons. If it is not within a PSI field, it will become "Disabled" and not function. Can be given to any unit. You can also disable it on Protoss buildings so they can be built anywhere.

UnitAdvRegenerate=Unit will slowly regain Hit Points (4/256 HP per frame) once completed, until its full HP capacity. Works for any race.

UnitAdvRobotic=Unit is treated as a robotic-type target: weapons with the Non-Robotic target flag (e.g. Spawn Broodlings) cannot hit it.

UnitAdvSpellcaster=Unit has energy: receives an energy bar and will slowly regain energy points through time. Combined with the Permanent Cloak property, will prevent unit from regaining energy.

UnitAdvSubunit=Makes the unit a "sub-unit", i.e. allows it to be a part of another unit (like the tank turret to the tank body) through the Subunit property (see Graphics tab). A non-subunit-type unit used as a subunit will crash the game. To have a sub-unit properly placed on a unit, you also require altering the images.dat properties of the base sprite as well as certain other settings. Expect crashes more than often while working with this property.

UnitAdvTwoInEgg=2 units will come out of one Zerg Egg instead of just one. The cost for morphing will NOT be doubled, but the amount of the used supplies will equal 2 times the normal amount. To accomplish the full effect you will also have to add a "Construction" graphics and set a "Landing Dust" overlay to it.

UnitAdvUnused=Unused: nothing in the game or StarEdit tests this flag.

UnitAdvWorker=Unit can gather/return resources and carry powerups and flags. Does NOT affect building construction abilities (except a tiny Drone glitch if you cancel a building morph). Requires a .LOO file pointed from the Special Overlay property in Images.dat to work. Vespene Gas harvesting seems good for all units, but Minerals may cause crashes, depending on the unit you use (e.g. Marine is OK, but the Firebat will crash)

UnitSub1=Main subunit to the unit, mostly turrets. 228 = None. Only created for units 0-105; if it cannot be created, the unit dies. The unit uses the subunit's weapons when it has none of its own, and its Target Acquisition Range. [pointer to units.dat]

UnitSub2=Not used: nothing in the game or StarEdit reads it. [pointer to units.dat]

UnitInfestation=Unit to transform into when a Queen infests the building. Exists only for units of ID 106-201 (buildings). The game only lets Queens infest the Command Center, so only its entry is used. [pointer to units.dat]
UnitInfestationList=Unit to transform into when a Queen infests the building. Exists only for units of ID 106-201 (buildings). The game only lets Queens infest the Command Center, so only its entry is used. [pointer to units.dat]

UnitMov01=Movement flags: the game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1). In StarEdit, a non-building with 0x01 must be placed where every tile under it can be walked on.

UnitMov02=Movement flags: no separate effect. The game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1: 0x01, 0x40 and 0x80): Spider Mines ignore such units, and they hatch 7 pixels higher from an egg.

UnitMov04=Movement flags: no separate effect. The game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1: 0x01, 0x40 and 0x80): Spider Mines ignore such units, and they hatch 7 pixels higher from an egg.

UnitMov08=Movement flags: no separate effect. The game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1: 0x01, 0x40 and 0x80): Spider Mines ignore such units, and they hatch 7 pixels higher from an egg.

UnitMov10=Movement flags: no separate effect. The game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1: 0x01, 0x40 and 0x80): Spider Mines ignore such units, and they hatch 7 pixels higher from an egg.

UnitMov20=Movement flags: no separate effect. The game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1: 0x01, 0x40 and 0x80): Spider Mines ignore such units, and they hatch 7 pixels higher from an egg.

UnitMov40=Movement flags: no separate effect. The game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1: 0x01, 0x40 and 0x80): Spider Mines ignore such units, and they hatch 7 pixels higher from an egg.

UnitMov80=Movement flags: no separate effect. The game only tests the whole value 193 (0xC1: 0x01, 0x40 and 0x80): Spider Mines ignore such units, and they hatch 7 pixels higher from an egg.

UnitReq=Position of the unit's entry in the unit requirement list built into StarCraft.exe; the requirements themselves cannot be edited in the dat files. Keep 65535: as soon as the game checks a unit holding 65535, it stores the position of every unit that has an entry in that list, replacing what the file had, and sets the rest to 0 (never allowed). Until then, and always if no unit holds 65535, values are used as they are: 0 = never allowed, any other value is read as requirements from that position in the list (the start of another unit's entry gives its requirements; the end of an entry gives none). Nothing checks it. See the Help for details.

[weapons Editor]

WeapDamageAmount=Damage of one hit, in hit points, before upgrades, armor and size. Irradiate (weapon 34) and Plague (weapon 60) read it as their total damage over time; the Nuclear Missile damage type ignores it. A map's unit settings can replace it.

WeapDamageBonus=Damage added for each level of the Damage Upgrade. A map's unit settings can replace it.

WeapDamageCooldown="Reload time": frames between two attacks. The game multiplies it by 1.25 when slowed (Ensnare) or 0.5 when sped up (Stim Packs, Adrenal Glands), adds a little for acid spores, keeps the result between 5 and 250 and then adds a random -1 to +2. 1 game second equals: on Fastest-24, on Faster-21, on Fast-18, on Normal-15, on Slow-12, on Slower-9 and on Slowest-6. A value of 0 makes the AI code divide by zero (crash).

WeapDamageExplosion=What happens when the bullet hits (for "Persist on Target Site" bullets, every 7 frames): damage the target, a splash, or a spell effect. Spells that are weapons (Lockdown, EMP Shockwave and so on) are cast through this. Mind Control, Feedback and value 23 have no handler and stop the game with a fatal error.

WeapDamageFactor=Only the value 2 does anything in the game: each attack fires a second bullet right after the first (e.g. Goliath and Scout air missiles, Valkyrie). Any other value fires one bullet. The status bar multiplies the shown damage by it (but always uses 2 for Firebat and Zealot and 1 for Valkyrie).

WeapUnused=This property does NOTHING. It was thought to be related to what technology is used together with the weapon, but it turned out to be just a designer's hint.

WeapDamageType=How the damage is scaled by the target's size, and whether armor applies. Normal does 100% to Small, Medium and Large. Explosive does 50% to Small and 75% to Medium. Concussive does 50% to Medium and 25% to Large. Ignore Armor does 100% and skips armor and shield upgrades. Independent does 0%, so each hit deals only the minimum of half a hit point (shields still take the full damage). Units of Independent size take half a hit point from every type.

WeapDamageUpgrade=The upgrade whose level multiplies the "Bonus" value. Its level is also shown on the weapon's icon. Upgrade 60 is used for no upgrade (nobody can research it); StarEdit hides the bonus for it.

WeapLabel=The name of the weapon, displayed when you highlight its icon in the control bar. [pointer to stat_txt.tbl]

WeapError=The line displayed when the weapon is to acquire an invalid target (e.g. attacking a Mutalisk with a ground-only weapon, like Flamethrower) [pointer to stat_txt.tbl]

WeapAttackAngle=How far the unit may face away from its target and still fire, in 256ths of a turn. 128 or more = any direction, 0 = must face it exactly. Lurkers ignore it. Workers also use their ground weapon's value when facing a building to repair or construct it.

WeapBehaviour=How the bullet is created and moves. "Fly & Don't Follow" goes to where the target was when it fired, "Fly & Follow" homes on it. "Fly", "Appear on Target Unit" and "Bounce" shots can miss (e.g. up a cliff), landing 30 pixels short and dealing no damage to the target.

WeapGraphics=The bullet the weapon fires; only the low 16 bits are used. 0 means no bullet: melee weapons (claws, blades, Psi Blades) use 0 and deal damage from the unit's iscript. [pointer to flingy.dat]
WeapGraphicsList=The bullet the weapon fires; only the low 16 bits are used. 0 means no bullet: melee weapons (claws, blades, Psi Blades) use 0 and deal damage from the unit's iscript. [pointer to flingy.dat]

WeapIcon=The icon shown for the weapon in a selected unit's status panel. [pointer to a frame in unit\cmdbtns\cmdicons.grp]
WeapIconList=The icon shown for the weapon in a selected unit's status panel. [pointer to a frame in unit\cmdbtns\cmdicons.grp]

WeapLaunchSpin=Turns the bullet's starting direction by this angle (128 = 180 degrees), to the left and to the right in turn for successive bullets, which makes homing missiles curve. 0 = straight.
